kinship_extended chain-only target relations:

1) grandfather_chain(G, C) :- father(G, P), father(P, C)
2) grandmother_chain(GM, C) :- mother(GM, P), father(P, C)
3) paternal_uncle_chain(U, N) :- brother(U, P), father(P, N)
4) maternal_aunt_chain(A, N) :- sister(A, P), mother(P, N)
5) cousin_chain(X, Y) :- father(PX, X), sibling(PX, PY), father(PY, Y)

Note:
- Target relations above are NOT used as primitive facts anywhere else in the dataset.
- They are intended to be inferable only from chain patterns.
